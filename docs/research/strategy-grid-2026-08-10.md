## Method

- Signals computed on bar *i*'s close, filled at bar *i+1*'s open. No look-ahead.
- Costs: STT, brokerage, exchange txn, GST, SEBI fee, stamp duty, and stochastic slippage from a seeded PRNG.
- Significance: stationary block bootstrap, 1000 resamples, mean block 10 days, 95% interval.
- Parameters are textbook defaults, identical across tickers — no per-ticker tuning.
